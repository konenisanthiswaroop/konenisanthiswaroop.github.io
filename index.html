01 / About

From microcontrollers to 5G base stations

I started in embedded systems, writing C firmware for sensors and microcontrollers. After a stint in enterprise support, where I learned incident handling and root-cause analysis, I moved into 5G NR software.

Today I work on Layer 2 (MAC and scheduler) features for 5G RAN: reading the 3GPP specification, designing the change, implementing it in C++, unit testing it, and verifying it end to end. I enjoy turning dense standards into reliable, tested code, and automating the repetitive parts of engineering with AI agents.
